# Meta Ad Compliance for Coaching / Info Offers

Meta reviews the **whole package**: primary text, headline, on-screen text, the
**spoken audio** of the video (transcribed), the thumbnail, and the destination
(landing page, lead form, or the Instagram profile for profile-visit ads). One
violation anywhere rejects the ad. Ads can also be re-reviewed and rejected
after approval at any time.

## The rules that actually get coaching ads rejected

1. **Unrealistic outcomes / income claims (Meta: Unrealistic Outcomes).**
   - No earnings figures: "$10k/month", "100k a month", "six figures", "7 figs",
     "made 12k in a week", "cleared 110k".
   - No implied speed or ease: "in 20 days", "overnight", "without posting",
     "guaranteed", "no experience needed".
   - Growth multiples count too: "5x'd", "doubled in 30 days, tripled in 60".
   - Luxury lifestyle as proof (sports cars, mansions framed as the result) adds risk.
   - Sell the **process and what the client learns/does**, not the payout.
2. **Personal attributes.** Don't assert or imply the viewer's financial status
   or struggles: avoid "Are you stuck at $10k?", "You're struggling to get
   clients", "Your revenue isn't growing". Talk about coaches in general or about
   the method ("Most coaches…", "Here's how positioning works…").
3. **Profanity** is prohibited in ads, **including spoken audio**. One swear in
   the video is enough. Copy can't fix it — re-cut or bleep the video.
4. **Testimonials** that center on money made are treated as income claims. Use
   testimonials about the experience, clarity, support, process ("He gives me
   clarity on my next steps", "He over-delivered"), not revenue or multiples.
5. **Destination.** For profile-visit/follower ads the IG profile *is* the
   landing page: bio, pinned posts and the bio link must also avoid income
   claims. For link ads the landing page must too.

## Copy patterns

| Risky | Safer |
|---|---|
| "Scale to $100k/month" | "Build the systems behind a coaching business that grows" |
| "Coaches stuck at $10–30k/month…" | "Most coaches who plateau run into the same three problems…" |
| "Josh 5x'd his business in 20 days" | "Josh shares what changed once his offer and systems were in place" |
| "Get clients without posting" | "Let your best content keep working after you post it" |
| "Are you struggling to get clients?" | "Here's how client acquisition works when it's a system" |
| "Guaranteed results" | (remove) |

Follower/profile-visit ads: keep copy short, value-led, and about *what they'll
learn by following* ("I post free breakdowns on content, positioning and sales
for coaches. Follow to get them in your feed.").

## Creative pre-flight (do this before uploading)

- Transcribe the video (Higgsfield video analysis) and scan the audio for:
  dollar amounts, "k a month", "figures", multiples, timeframes tied to money,
  guarantees, swearing, drugs/addiction references.
- Check on-screen text overlays the same way.
- Prefer creatives that already passed review on the same Page/IG account.

## Handling rejections (protect the account)

- Every disapproval is recorded against the account; editing or deleting a
  rejected ad doesn't erase it. Repeated disapprovals can restrict the account.
- **Don't blindly resubmit** rejected ads. Pause them, read the exact policy in
  Ads Manager (hover "Rejected") or Business Support Home → Account Quality.
- If the rejection is clearly wrong, request review in Account Quality instead of
  duplicating the ad.
- Fix root causes (creative audio, destination) before launching new versions;
  launch the fixed version as new ads in a fresh ad set, not mass re-edits.
